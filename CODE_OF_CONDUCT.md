# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
impersonation and deliberate exposure of personal information are not welcome.
These expectations apply to issues, pull requests and other project discussions.

Health information is especially sensitive. Never post real reports, patient
screenshots, contact details, credentials or private production logs. Use
fabricated reproductions. Do not present this software as medical diagnosis
or promise that a negative check guarantees the absence of disease.

Maintainers may edit or remove unsafe material, close discussions or restrict
participation, proportionately and subject to the hosting platform's controls.
Report security or privacy exposure according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
For conduct concerns, use a private maintainer/platform reporting channel when
available; do not repost the sensitive content in a public complaint.

The project does not guarantee immediate responses or complete deletion of
third-party caches. Revoke exposed credentials promptly.
